# Scenario : Healthcare — Hospital Patient & Treatment Analytics

## Business Scenario

A multi-specialty hospital wants to build a **Data Warehouse** to analyze patient visits, treatments, doctors, departments, and billing information.

The hospital currently stores operational data in different systems:

* Patient registration system
* Appointment system
* Doctor/treatment system
* Pharmacy system
* Billing system

Management wants a centralized Data Warehouse that can help them understand **hospital performance, patient trends, treatment costs, and doctor/department performance**.

### Business Requirements

The management wants to answer questions such as:

1. How many patients visited the hospital each month?
2. What is the total revenue generated by each department?
3. What is the average treatment cost per patient?
4. Which doctors handled the highest number of patients?
5. Which department has the highest number of admissions?
6. What is the average length of hospital stay?
7. What is the total revenue generated from different treatment types?
8. How many patients are returning patients?
9. What is the monthly treatment/revenue trend?
10. Which age group has the highest number of hospital visits?
11. What is the cancellation rate for appointments?
12. Which departments have the highest treatment costs?
